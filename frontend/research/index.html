VERIFICATION BOUNDARY

What this view establishes

A signed presentation of the last observed evidence, retained without a five-minute display cutoff. The history algorithms operate on that observed copy.

  • Accepted personnel state is not established.
  • Journal observations do not prove all source changes were captured.
  • Independent deployment and public finality are not established by this view.
