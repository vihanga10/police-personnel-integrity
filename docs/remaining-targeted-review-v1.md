registered archive, confirmation, file-header, row-provenance, SQL target and
primary/backup key-recovery checks. No evidence or classification is changed.

complaints, courts to operations/complaints, and complaints to courts. The
complaint linked_case_no comparison with operation_no is provisional; its meaning
requires source confirmation. Transfers, punishment and HRC references remain
outside this review. Missing/mismatched keys are observations, not final findings.
